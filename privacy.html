Privacy Policy for FormVault QA

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

FormVault QA stores and replays all saved form profiles and synthetic QA test data 100% locally inside your browser.

1. Password Safety & Zero Cloud Uploads

  • FormVault QA explicitly ignores password fields (input[type="password"]) during both form capture and dummy data filling.
  • We never collect, transmit, or sell your form entries or browsing history.

2. Permissions

  • activeTab & scripting: Used strictly when you click Save Current Form, Fill, or Fill Form with QA Data on the currently active tab.
  • storage: Stores your saved profiles and optional Pro license status locally in chrome.storage.local.
  • https://api.gumroad.com/*: Contacted solely when you voluntarily enter a FormVault QA Pro license key and click Activate.
